Purva Land is the dedicated plotted development arm of Puravankara Limited. Committed to delivering premium lifestyle experiences, the entity specialises in theme-based developments. It offers world-class infrastructure, strategic locations with clear titles, and thoughtfully planned layouts to meet the needs of both investors and end users.

In just five years, Purva Land has established a strong presence across Bengaluru, Chennai, and Coimbatore. The company has successfully delivered 9 projects spanning 4.6 msft and secured a land bank of over 10 msft for future developments.

Each Purva Land project is uniquely conceptualised around a central theme—be it wellness, sustainability, or luxury—offering every development a distinct identity and purpose. These themes are brought to life through curated amenities, lush green spaces, and innovative design elements, ensuring unparalleled value beyond the land itself.

Purva Land has successfully delivered several prestigious projects, including Purva Woodfield, Purva Tree Haven, and Purva Tivoli Hills in Bengaluru, along with Purva Southbay in Chennai and Purva Hibiscus in Coimbatore. The brand continues its commitment to excellence with ongoing projects such as Purva Oakshire and Purva Kensho Hills in Bengaluru, as well as Purva Raagam and Purva Soukhyam in Chennai, showcasing its extensive footprint across India’s major cities.
