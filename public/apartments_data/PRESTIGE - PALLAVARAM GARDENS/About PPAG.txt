ABOUT PPAG

Pallavaram is considered to be the oldest inhabited place in South India. Today it is the fastest developing and most booming of Chennai’s suburbs. A home here thus enables you to enjoy both the pride of historic stature and the convenience of contemporary infrastructure and amenities.

And what better place to make your home than Prestige Pallavaram Gardens, a premium enclave of 2069 two-bed, three-bed and four-bed homes, that adds to to Pallavaram the unmistakable Prestige stamp of quality and class.

ENJOY EXCELLENT ACCESS AND CONNECTIVITY

Pallavaram is one of the best-connected of Chennai’s neighbourhoods. It is connected by both Old Mahabalipuram Road and Pallavaram-Thuraipakkam Radial Road. Other arterial roads such as East Coast Road and the Outer Ring Road are close by. And the Airport is just 20 minutes away via the Grand Southern Trunk Road (NH45).

LIVE THE EASY COMMUTE LIFE

Bid goodbye to commuting headaches as your home in Prestige Pallavaram Gardens is a short and easy drive from work.

Prominent IT Parks, like the Chennai Trade Centre and Olympia Tech Park, and the SIDCO Industrial Estate are all just half an hour away. And major localities like Velachery and Guindy, which house several major tech parks and SEZs are nearby as well.

EXPERIENCE CONVENIENCE AT YOUR FINGERTIPS

Pallavaram is also home to the full range of high profile social amenities, including premium schools, shopping centres, hotels, restaurants, healthcare facilities and more. Life at Prestige Pallavaram Gardens will thus be a smooth, unhurried experience, with everything you need so close to you, and giving you more time to enjoy them.
