Puravankara Limited, headquartered in Bengaluru, is one of India’s most admired and trusted real estate developers, with a legacy of excellence spanning over 50 years. Founded in 1975, the company is known for delivering high-quality homes on time with complete transparency.

Puravankara has launched three prominent brands, each catering to distinct market segments. Puravankara itself is recognized for its luxurious, state-of-the-art projects that incorporate innovative technology to redefine future homes. Provident Housing Ltd., a wholly-owned subsidiary launched in 2008, addresses the needs of mid-income homeowners. Purva Land, established in 2021, offers a lucrative investment opportunity through plotted developments, responding to the evolving needs of homebuyers. Additionally, Starworth Infrastructure and Construction Limited (SICL) provides technology-enabled construction solutions. Puravankara also has a strong presence in commercial office spaces.

With a footprint in Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Kochi, Goa, Kolkata, Coimbatore, and Mangaluru, Puravankara was among the first developers in India to secure Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in real estate. The company has delivered 86+ residential and commercial projects, totaling over 50 million square feet of development.

Puravankara has garnered numerous prestigious awards, including the e4m Pride of India – The Best of Bharat Awards 2022, 12th Realty+ Excellence Awards 2021 - Best Themed Project of the Year (Purva Emerald Bay), and the 13th Annual Estate Awards 2021 – Developer of the Year (Residential South). It has also received accolades such as the India Property Awards 2021 - Real Estate Thought Leader of the Year for Ashish Puravankara, recognition among the top 20 for Innovation in the Women in Corporate Awards 2019 for Amanda Puravankara, and the Lifetime Achievement Award by CREDAI Karnataka 2023 for Chairman Ravi Puravankara. Further recognition includes the ET Edge Iconic Brands of India Award 2023, Most Admired Company in Urban Infrastructure Award by ET Infra Focus, and the prestigious Vishwakarma Award for Social Development and Impact from the Construction Industry Development Council (CIDC).

Puravankara Ltd. continues to disrupt the realty segment with its professional services, high governance standards, and a commitment to excellence.
